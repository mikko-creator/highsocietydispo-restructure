Michigan Dispensary FAQ

High Society’s answers from its store pages. More questions? Visit our FAQ page.

What are the requirements to purchase from High Society?

To shop at our dispensary, you need a valid government-issued ID proving you are 21 years or older. Individuals 18 and older with a medical marijuana card can also make purchases.

How should I handle a defective product?

If you experience issues with a product's quality or functionality, please call your store or visit the store where you made the purchase. Our team is happy to help.

What payment methods are supported at High Society dispensaries?

High Society accepts cash and debit cards. Many locations also feature an in-store ATM for your convenience. Some stores may offer additional payment options—call ahead for details.

Is it legal to travel with cannabis?

Cannabis purchases are legal in Michigan, but transporting cannabis across state lines is prohibited under federal law. Avoid taking cannabis products out of the state.

What types of cannabis products are available?

We offer a wide range of high-quality products, including flower, pre-rolls, concentrates, vape pens, edibles, and more. Check out our menu or contact us with specific inquiries.

Are there any rewards programs for regular customers?

Absolutely! Join The Society, our loyalty program, to start earning points on your purchases. Points can be redeemed for exclusive discounts and offers. Speak to our staff for more information.

Experience convenience at your fingertips with the High Society App. Easily browse our full cannabis menu, stay updated on the latest deals, and place your orders with just a few taps. Whether you're at home or on the go, managing your cannabis experience has never been easier.
