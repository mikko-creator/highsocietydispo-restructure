Gluten-Free Chocolate Cherry Tarts, Infused Three Ways

Make-ahead mini chocolate tarts on a buckwheat crust with a tart cherry glaze. Infuse the crust, the ganache or the topping, then total the mg and divide.

By the High Society teamPublished October 24, 20263 min read

03min readRecipes
In this article
  1. Dosing a cannabis chocolate tart: pick your infusion point
  2. Ingredients and method
  3. Serving
  4. Storage and labeling
  5. Dairy-free version
  6. Recipe
  7. Frequently asked questions

Key takeaways

  • You can infuse the crust butter, the ganache or the whipped topping; start with just one.
  • Add up the THC from every infused layer, then divide by the number of tarts or wedges.
  • Buckwheat is naturally gluten-free, but flour can pick up gluten from shared equipment, so check labels.
  • Keep the tarts covered, labeled and refrigerated, and eat them within about three days.

This cannabis chocolate tart recipe makes six make-ahead minis: a cocoa-buckwheat crust, silky dark chocolate ganache, a tart cherry glaze and a spoonful of mascarpone cream. It's a gluten-free cannabis dessert, and you decide where the dose lives.

Dosing a cannabis chocolate tart: pick your infusion point

Infusion pointHow to add itGood to know
CrustSwap part of the cold butter for cold cannabutterBakes about 15 minutes
GanacheStir melted cannabutter into the hot creamEasiest to mix evenly
ToppingWhip infused cream with the mascarponeNeeds equal spoonfuls

Start with one point; an infused ganache mixes most evenly. Infusing more than one layer? Add the totals. With cannabutter at about 30 mg per tablespoon, 1 tablespoon in the ganache gives about 5 mg per tart. Add the same to the crust and each tart reaches about 10 mg, the most a licensed adult-use edible may hold per serving in Michigan.

Have half a tart first and wait 2 hours, since effects can take that long to show. Our THC dosage guide explains starting amounts.

Ingredients and method

The buckwheat tart crust is buckwheat flour, cornstarch, cocoa, sugar and salt rubbed with cold butter, then bound with vanilla and a splash of cold water or cherry juice. With no gluten to hold it together, press it into the pans by hand instead of rolling. Chill, bake at 350°F and cool. Baking for someone who avoids gluten? Choose flours labeled gluten-free and check the cocoa and chocolate too.

For the ganache, pour steaming cream over chopped dark chocolate, wait a minute and whisk until glossy, then fill the shells and chill. Simmer tart cherry juice with sugar into a syrup, and whip cream with mascarpone to soft peaks. Our cannabutter recipe covers the infusion, and our budtenders can help you choose flower at your nearest store.

Serving

Top each tart with a drizzle of cherry syrup, one spoonful of topping and fresh cherries or berries. If the whipped cream is infused, use the same spoon for every tart.

Storage and labeling

Cover the tarts, mark the container with the date and estimated mg per tart, and refrigerate it away from plain desserts. They're freshest within about three days. Keep them on a shelf kids and pets can't reach, and after an accidental bite, call poison control at 1-800-222-1222.

Dairy-free version

Use stick-style plant butter in the crust and canna-coconut oil as the infusion. Make the ganache with warm full-fat coconut cream and dairy-free chocolate, and whip chilled coconut cream for the topping. Find more ideas in our infused recipes.

These tarts are for adults 21 and older who know they're infused.

Recipe

Makes
6 mini tarts (4-inch) or one 9-inch tart
Prep time
40 minutes
Cook time
35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup buckwheat flour
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 cup sugar, plus 3 tablespoons for the glaze
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, cubed (swap cold cannabutter in for part of it to infuse the crust)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 to 4 tablespoons cold water or cherry juice
  • 8 ounces dark chocolate (60 to 70%), finely chopped
  • 1 cup heavy cream, for the ganache
  • Melted cannabutter for the ganache, measured to your dose (optional)
  • 1 cup tart cherry juice, for the glaze
  • 1/2 cup cold heavy cream (or chilled infused cream), for the topping
  • 1/4 cup mascarpone
  • 1 tablespoon powdered sugar
  • Fresh cherries and berries, for serving

Steps

  1. Whisk the buckwheat flour, cornstarch, cocoa, 1/4 cup sugar and salt. Rub in the cold butter (and any cannabutter) with your fingertips until the mix looks like coarse crumbs.

  2. Stir in the vanilla and just enough cold water or juice for the crumbs to hold when squeezed. Divide evenly among six 4-inch tart pans, press into the bottoms and sides, prick with a fork and chill for 30 minutes.

  3. Heat the oven to 350°F. Bake the shells for 15 to 18 minutes, until firm and fragrant, then cool completely.

  4. Heat 1 cup cream until steaming and stir in any measured cannabutter. Pour it over the chopped chocolate, wait 1 minute and whisk until smooth. Divide evenly among the shells and chill until set, at least 2 hours.

  5. Simmer the cherry juice with 3 tablespoons sugar for 15 to 20 minutes, until it reduces to about 1/3 cup. It thickens as it cools.

  6. Whip the cold cream, mascarpone and powdered sugar to soft peaks.

  7. To serve, drizzle each tart with cherry syrup, add one equal spoonful of topping and finish with fresh fruit.

Dosing note

Read these numbers as estimates. Example: 1 tablespoon of cannabutter at about 30 mg in the ganache gives 30 ÷ 6 tarts ≈ 5 mg per tart at most. Infuse the crust the same way and it's about 10 mg per tart. Actual THC per tart depends on your flower's potency and on what decarboxylation and infusion lost along the way. Start with half a tart and wait at least 2 hours before having more. Label the tarts as infused, and keep them away from children and pets.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make infused cream?

For ganache, stir a measured spoonful of melted cannabutter into the warm cream. For whipping, steep decarbed flower in heavy cream over very low heat for about an hour without letting it boil, strain it, and chill it overnight. Label infused cream and use it within a couple of days.

Is buckwheat gluten-free?

Yes, naturally; despite its name, buckwheat isn't wheat. The catch is cross-contact: gluten can get in when it's harvested or processed on equipment shared with wheat. Celiac groups suggest products labeled or tested gluten-free, and no bulk bins. Baking for someone with celiac disease? Check every ingredient, not just the flour.

Can I make one large tart instead?

Yes. Press the crust into a 9-inch tart pan and bake it for 20 to 25 minutes, until it looks dry and smells toasty. Fill and chill as usual, mark 8 equal wedges before cutting, and divide the total mg by 8 instead of 6.
