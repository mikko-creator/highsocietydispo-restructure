What every store has in common

A few things hold at all eight locations, so a first visit anywhere goes smoothly.

  • Bring your ID

    Adult-use shopping is for 21 and older: bring a valid government-issued ID or passport and check in at the front desk. Registered medical patients bring their card too. 21+ only. Please consume responsibly.

  • Paying

    Cash works at every store, and each store page lists an ATM in-store. Other payment options vary by store, so call ahead for details.

  • Order ahead

    Shop in store, or order online and choose a pickup time for in-store or curbside pickup. Curbside customers get a text when the order is ready.

  • Join The Society

    Our loyalty program is free. Members earn points on qualifying purchases and redeem them in store or online. See how it works.

Coming from Indiana, Chicago or Harbor Country?

Our New Buffalo store sits on US-12, minutes from I-94 Exit 1 and just over the Indiana line. These pages cover routes, parking and quick-stop tips. Whichever way you come, cannabis bought in Michigan stays in Michigan.
